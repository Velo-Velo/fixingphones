    Questions
    
      
    
        Do I need to give you my passcode?
        
    It helps us test everything after the repair, like the camera, Face ID and touch. If you'd rather not share it, we'll test what we can without it and you can check the rest when it's back.
    
      
    
      
    
        Do I need to turn off Find My iPhone?
        
    Not for most repairs. If a repair needs it turned off, we'll tell you before we start.
    
      
    
      
    
        Will I lose my photos and data?
        
    Repairs like screens, batteries and charging ports don't touch your data, but it's always worth backing up first. We never look through your photos, messages or files.
    
      
    
      
    
        What if you can't fix it?
        
    You pay nothing, not even the collection fee.
